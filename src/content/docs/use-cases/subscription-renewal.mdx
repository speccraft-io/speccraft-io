title: Canceled plan renewed
description: The renewal job also renews plans that are canceling, so a canceled plan is charged and active again.

A customer can cancel a plan at any time. The plan runs to the end of the paid period, and until then the customer
can resume it. At the end of each period a renewal job charges every live plan. "Live" was taken to include plans
that are canceling, so a plan canceled during the period is charged again and made active at the next renewal.
