A ticket shop lets a customer hold a seat, then buy it or let it go, and a sold seat can be refunded. Each customer
may have one seat. The hold is allowed when the seat is free and the customer holds no other seat. The count looks
only at held seats, so once a customer buys their seat, nothing stops them from holding a second one.
