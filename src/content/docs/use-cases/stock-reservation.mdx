A shop holds stock for a customer while they check out. `reserve` checks that there is enough stock, saves the
reservation, then takes the units out of stock. The check runs before the `await` and the units are taken after it,
so two reservations that start together both pass the check against the same stock. The check also lets a quantity
of 0, a negative one or a fraction through.
