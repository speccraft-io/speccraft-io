A cart reducer takes the cart and an action and returns the new cart. Every item costs 10.00, and the `SAVE5`
coupon needs a total of at least 20.00. The minimum is checked only when the coupon is applied: add two items, apply
the coupon, remove one item, and the coupon stays on a 10.00 cart.
