A bill in cents is split between people. Everyone pays the same share, rounded up so nobody underpays, and the last
person pays what is left. For 1,000 cents between 3 people it gives 334, 334 and 332. But with small shares and many
people, rounding up takes more than the whole bill before the last person is reached, and the last share is
negative.
