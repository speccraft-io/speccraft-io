What does demand for formal methods skills look like these days? Is it worth learning these tools for a job, and who
would hire you? Real, current hiring concentrates in two places: large-company research labs hiring for Lean/Coq/TLA+
expertise, and defense/government contractors requiring US citizenship and clearance.
