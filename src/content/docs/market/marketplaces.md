*Last checked: September 20, 2026.*

Where does formal methods work show up outside full-time jobs? Each platform below was searched for formal methods,
TLA+, model checking and related terms. "Relevance" rates how much real, on-topic work was found there, from ★★★ (the
most) to ☆☆☆ (nothing).

## Platforms

### LinkedIn ★★★

[linkedin.com](https://www.linkedin.com)

By far the richest job-posting source: Sigil Logic, Architect, Alignerr (Lean 4, x2), a Principal Research Engineer role in Ireland explicitly building "a TLA+ model to verify transaction [correctness]" for large-scale distributed systems, 13+ formal methods researcher roles, ABB thesis work.

Conclusion: Real, substantial, current hiring demand exists, concentrated in research-engineer/principal-level full-time roles, not gig/freelance work.

Evidence: [search used](https://www.google.com/search?q=%22TLA%2B%22+OR+%22model+checking%22+jobs+site:linkedin.com/jobs)

### Wellfound ★★★

[wellfound.com](https://wellfound.com)

Richest signal of all platforms checked: multiple current startup job posts wanting formal methods experience - Axiomise (formal-methods-founded company), an "AI-Driven Compilation" role wanting SMT solvers/verified compilation, Tangram Flex (citing Galois's formal-methods reputation), a role citing "formal methods for guaranteeing convergence in distributed training."

Conclusion: Real, current demand from funded startups, closer to genuine engineering roles than any other marketplace checked. Not freelance/consulting though - these are hiring for employees.

Evidence: [search used](https://www.google.com/search?q=%22formal+methods%22+OR+%22TLA%2B%22+site:peopleperhour.com+OR+site:wellfound.com+OR+site:weworkremotely.com+OR+site:remoteok.com)

### Contra ★★☆

[contra.com](https://contra.com)

One real, current, specific freelancer listing: "Efficient Lean 4 Verification & Formal Methods for Software", citing a completed formal-methods build and public Lean work.

Conclusion: Real, active positioning by at least one practitioner - the most on-topic single listing found on any generalist platform.

Evidence: [search used](https://www.google.com/search?q=%22formal+methods%22+OR+%22TLA%2B%22+site:turing.com+OR+site:x-team.com+OR+site:contra.com+OR+site:guru.com)

### Toptal ★★☆

[toptal.com](https://www.toptal.com)

No dedicated formal-methods/verification category page. Individual profiles list "Formal Methods" as a skill (senior devs, one hardware/RTL verification profile).

Conclusion: Exists only as a supply-side skill tag, not a market category. Platform's premium/senior positioning fits better than Fiverr/Upwork, but no client-demand evidence found.

Evidence: [search used](https://www.google.com/search?q=Toptal+%22formal+methods%22+OR+%22TLA%2B%22+freelancer)

### web3.career ★★

[web3.career](https://web3.career)

Dedicated Web3/crypto job board with a "Research Jobs" tag; confirmed real listings for Formal Verification Engineer roles at Nethermind and Researcher/Engineer (Formal Verification) at Ethereum Foundation.

Conclusion: Not on the original general-marketplace list, found via crypto-specific research - a real, working niche aggregator for this exact skill, unlike the generalist platforms above.

Evidence: [Nethermind remote jobs page](https://web3.career/web3-companies/nethermind-remote)

### cryptocurrencyjobs.co ★★

[cryptocurrencyjobs.co](https://cryptocurrencyjobs.co)

Dedicated crypto job board + weekly newsletter; confirmed real listings for Formal Verification Engineer (Nethermind) and Researcher/Engineer (Formal Verification) (Ethereum Foundation).

Conclusion: Same category as web3.career - a real niche channel specifically for this skill in the crypto/Web3 space.

Evidence: [Nethermind FV listing](https://cryptocurrencyjobs.co/engineering/nethermind-formal-verification-engineer/)

### PeoplePerHour ★☆☆

[peopleperhour.com](https://www.peopleperhour.com)

Real posted job: "Formal Methods Developer," fixed price ~$121. Real freelancer profile listing "Formal Methods (C): Software Verification with model checker Spin, B method." Same "Terminal Bench Expert" AI-eval gig as Turing also appears here.

Conclusion: Real but small and low-priced; one genuine specialist profile exists.

Evidence: [search used](https://www.google.com/search?q=%22formal+methods%22+OR+%22TLA%2B%22+site:peopleperhour.com+OR+site:wellfound.com+OR+site:weworkremotely.com+OR+site:remoteok.com)

### Freelancer.com ★☆☆

[freelancer.com](https://www.freelancer.com)

Real posted jobs, but old and low-value: "Formal Methods Task" ($10-30), "Z specifications formal methods homework" ($30-250) - academic assignment work, not production consulting. Also hardware-verification hire pages mentioning formal methods (UVM).

Conclusion: Real but low-value, mostly student/homework tasks, not a serious channel.

Evidence: [search used](https://www.google.com/search?q=%22formal+methods%22+OR+%22TLA%2B%22+site:freelancer.com+OR+site:gun.io+OR+site:arc.dev+OR+site:lemon.io)

### Turing ★☆☆

[turing.com](https://www.turing.com)

"Terminal Bench Expert" gig lists "Formal Methods & Theorem Proving" as one of many skill tags. This is AI-eval/RLHF task work (rating AI outputs), not engineering consulting.

Conclusion: Real but wrong kind of work - paid AI-training tasks, not consulting.

Evidence: [search used](https://www.google.com/search?q=%22formal+methods%22+OR+%22TLA%2B%22+site:turing.com+OR+site:x-team.com+OR+site:contra.com+OR+site:guru.com)

### Arc.dev ★☆☆

[arc.dev](https://arc.dev)

One real company profile sourcing through Arc: "Generation Lambda," building decentralized finance protocols "using formal methods, quantitative modelling, and functional programming." One freelancer profile mentions TLA+/Dafny in passing.

Conclusion: Thin but real: at least one genuine defi employer values formal methods here.

Evidence: [search used](https://www.google.com/search?q=%22formal+methods%22+OR+%22TLA%2B%22+site:freelancer.com+OR+site:gun.io+OR+site:arc.dev+OR+site:lemon.io)

### Fiverr ★☆☆

[fiverr.com](https://www.fiverr.com)

"TLA+", "model checking", "formal verification", "distributed systems consulting", "concurrency bug", "race condition", "protocol design review", "software correctness" all return thousands of results, all irrelevant noise (music mixing, 3D rendering, ID verification, career coaching). "Smart contract audit" returns real, relevant, reviewed sellers at $57-$152.

Conclusion: No market for formal methods. Adjacent smart contract audit niche is real and functioning.

Evidence: [TLA+ (null)](https://www.fiverr.com/search/gigs?query=TLA%2B) · [smart contract audit (real)](https://www.fiverr.com/search/gigs?query=smart%20contract%20audit)

### Upwork ★☆☆

[upwork.com](https://www.upwork.com)

"TLA+" has zero indexed pages on upwork.com. "Formal methods" has 3 results, one being a freelancer offering formal-methods-based LLM reasoning validation. "Smart contract audit" has many real freelancers, job posts ($1,000 range), a dedicated rate page.

Conclusion: Same as Fiverr: no market for formal methods, smart contract audit is real, small adjacent niche in LLM/AI output validation.

Evidence: [TLA+ (null)](https://www.google.com/search?q=site:upwork.com+%22TLA%2B%22) · [smart contract audit (real)](https://www.google.com/search?q=site:upwork.com+%22smart+contract+audit%22)
